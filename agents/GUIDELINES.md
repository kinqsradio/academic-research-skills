# Collaboration roles

Use [README.md](README.md) for role responsibilities, bounded delegation, task packets, and handoff receipts. Roles describe work, not specific models. Keep reusable research procedures in `skills/` and actual assignments, including session-specific model and effort choices, in `docs/planning/`; start with [model routing](routing/MODEL_ROUTING.md), then read [Codex](routing/CODEX.md) or [Claude](routing/CLAUDE.md) for the chosen host's model and effort limits. Keep provider details out of the shared index.

For substantial work with independent parts, use delegated workers under the user's standing authorization, subject to host limits and the default concurrency cap in `README.md`. Keep simple or serial tasks with one contributor. Assign separate output ownership and avoid nested fanout by default.

Do not claim independent peer review from repeated calls or agreement among assistants. Record the actual reviewer arrangement; isolated assistant workers can provide separate review contexts but do not prove uncorrelated errors.
