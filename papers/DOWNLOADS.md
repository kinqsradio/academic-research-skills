# Paper download register

This template contains no downloaded papers. On a research branch, record every download attempt under [paper guidance](GUIDELINES.md) and maintain verified IEEE entries in [REFERENCES.md](REFERENCES.md). Preserve unknown fields explicitly. Source IDs identify works; register additional versions under the same ID with distinct filenames and version notes.

| Source ID | Local filename | Verified title / authors / year | DOI or landing URL | Retrieval URL / date / downloader | Version / access / verification status | SHA-256 / bytes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

PDFs stay local and ignored on every branch. Commit this metadata on the research branch; preserve it when files are absent. Compute size and SHA-256 for successful local downloads.
