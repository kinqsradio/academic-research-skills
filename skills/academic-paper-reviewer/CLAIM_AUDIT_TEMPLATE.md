## Claim checks

| Claim ID | Manuscript location and precise assertion | Type: finding / interpretation / hypothesis / proposed work | Source ID and version | Source locator and reading scope | Assessment | Reason and required revision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Assessment vocabulary: supported, needs narrowing, contradicted, not evidenced in examined material, inaccessible, not yet checked. Inaccessible material is not a contradiction. A summary that omits a detail cannot establish that the original source lacks it. Check each source in a multi-source claim separately. Retain important contrary evidence. Quote only brief necessary passages; otherwise use faithful paraphrases with locators.

For numerical claims, record population, comparison, outcome, time point, denominator, units, estimate, uncertainty, and the actual table/figure/analysis location. Do not infer an exact number from a plot. Keep source identity checks separate from claim support.

## Exhibits and reproducibility

| Exhibit ID | File and caption | Manuscript callout | Data or source locator | Producing script / command / manual step | Value and uncertainty checks | Render check | Open issue |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Check that labels and callouts resolve; check that text, table, caption, and plotted values describe the same sample and analysis. Record units and accessibility issues from an actual render, or mark rendering unperformed. An existing image does not prove that the analysis ran. Do not overwrite reference outputs during a reproduction attempt.
