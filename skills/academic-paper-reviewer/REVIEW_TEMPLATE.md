## Overall assessment

Describe the central contribution supported by the draft and the issues most likely to change its interpretation. Do not supply a publication verdict or numeric grade unless specifically requested with a supplied basis.

## Findings

| ID | Priority | Manuscript location | Problem and evidence | Why it matters | Suggested correction |

For prose and punctuation findings, distinguish grammatical/meaning errors, explicit requirement breaches, house-style inconsistencies, and optional clarity suggestions. State the rule and effect on the reader; punctuation or vocabulary alone does not establish AI authorship.

Use stable finding IDs for a later re-review. Major and minor findings describe demonstrated problems; optional suggestions should be clearly separate. Quote only enough manuscript text to locate the issue and include the relevant source locator when checking a claim.

## Validation and limitations

State what references, figures, calculations, requirements, or analyses were checked. For IEEE, record first-citation order, repeated-source numbering, reference-list correspondence, and metadata checks. Link source identities and provenance to the [reference catalog](../../papers/REFERENCES.md) and [download register](../../papers/DOWNLOADS.md); paper IDs are independent of manuscript reference numbers. List inaccessible sources and unperformed reproduction work. Distinguish an evidence gap from a verified contradiction.
