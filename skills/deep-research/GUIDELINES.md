# Deep-research skill guidelines

This is an original, team-owned workflow for scholarly source discovery and evidence synthesis. Keep it in English and usable in Codex, Claude, and other capable environments without requiring a named model, provider, account, or API.

`SKILL.md` is the entrypoint. The search-record and evidence-matrix templates support reproducibility and claim verification; keep their relative links valid. This skill produces evidence syntheses in `docs/literature/`. Manuscript prose belongs to `academic-paper`, and protocol-based study eligibility decisions belong to `sr-screener`.

Add or change guidance only when it improves a recurring research decision. Write guidance in the team's own words and avoid unnecessary runtime assumptions. Keep source attribution, precise evidence locators, uncertainty, and the distinction between findings and interpretation explicit.

Use the canonical [paper guidance](../../papers/GUIDELINES.md), [download register](../../papers/DOWNLOADS.md), and [reference catalog](../../papers/REFERENCES.md) for source identity and provenance. Keep stable paper IDs separate from artifact-specific IEEE citation numbers. Update relevant supporting records when changing this workflow. The lead coordinates and checks scoped worker outputs; workers do not delegate further. Human-selected lead model and effort remain outside subagent routing limits.
