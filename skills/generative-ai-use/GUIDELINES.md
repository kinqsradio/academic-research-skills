# Generative AI use disclosure

This folder maintains one reusable workflow for evidence-based generative AI use disclosures. Read [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) as the entrypoint from either assistant. Use [APPENDIX_TEMPLATE.md](APPENDIX_TEMPLATE.md) for the author-facing appendix: tool/principle, stage-by-stage AI and author workflow, evidenced controls, and labelled sample interactions. Use [USE_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md](USE_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md) for the supporting working record.

Keep these resources project-neutral and empty of actual author declarations or private transcripts. Put populated working records in an appropriate purpose-based `docs/` subfolder on the research branch; put disclosure drafts beside the manuscript in `submissions/drafts/<topic>/`. Follow each destination's guidance and link the records through safe repository-relative references. Restricted records stay in an authorized access-controlled location outside Git; record only a safe reference and its access limits.

Keep the [skill index](../GUIDELINES.md) and adjacent writing/review workflows consistent with this skill. Validate structure and exercise representative disclosure cases separately; neither establishes course compliance or human approval.

Derive stages from actual records rather than imposing a fixed list. Keep sample provenance and internal readiness/confirmation details in the working record; the appendix should read as a specific author account, with material limits preserved. Follow any mandatory alternative declaration format and record the reason. Supplied examples guide structure and voice, never project facts or completed checks.
