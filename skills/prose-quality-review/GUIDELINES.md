# Prose-quality review skill

Maintain one focused, original review workflow here. [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) is the entrypoint for requests such as “check for slop”; [REVIEW_CRITERIA.md](REVIEW_CRITERIA.md) gives decision tests, negative cases and bounded research rationale; [REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md](REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md) defines actionable findings. Keep it readable by path in either assistant without proprietary APIs or automatic-registration claims.

The shared [argument and revision guide](../academic-paper/argument-and-revision.md#clear-specific-prose) owns prose and punctuation standards. This skill owns the focused review procedure, not a competing writing manual, authorship detector, full evidence audit, or rewriting workflow. Keep manuscript reviews in `submissions/reviews/<topic>/` when file output is requested or established. Do not store project records here.

When maintaining the skill, check both meaningful flaws and clean technical/disciplinary prose. Do not treat formal language, protected source material, or a valid convention as a defect. Preserve evidence limits and disclosures. Update the parent index through the integrating owner; follow [skill-creator](../skill-creator/SKILL.md) for maintenance and distinguish structural validation, behavioral exercises, and live invocation.
