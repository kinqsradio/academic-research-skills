# Decision tests and evidence limits

Use these tests alongside the canonical [clear-prose](../academic-paper/argument-and-revision.md#clear-specific-prose) and [punctuation](../academic-paper/argument-and-revision.md#punctuation-by-function) standards. The categories are prompts for judgment, not diagnostic features or a score. Report a problem only when its location, effect and support can be explained.

| Examine | Actionable when | Preserve / do not infer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vagueness and unsupported specificity | The reader cannot identify a necessary actor, comparison, condition or referent; or a precise claim exceeds examined evidence. Name what is missing and what would establish it. | Unknown facts stay unknown. Missing source access makes support unverified, not disproved. |
| Low information and padding | Repetition or an opening/closing sentence adds no definition, reasoning, qualification, signposting or required summary in this genre. | Repeated scientific terms can prevent ambiguity; abstracts and conclusions legitimately repeat key results for different purposes. |
| Formulaic framing | A stock contrast, balanced list or transition takes space without stating the actual relationship, or imposes a relationship the passage does not support. | A conventional structure that communicates a real comparison, limitation or sequence is useful. |
| Argument continuity | A conclusion introduces an unexplained premise, a causal claim follows only an association, or a transition contradicts the stated inference boundary. | Do not invent causal mechanisms, experiments, examples or cross-references. A justified hedge is necessary precision. |
| Literature synthesis | The passage's purpose requires comparison or synthesis, but it only lists studies without relating their designs, populations, measures, findings or limits. | A valid isolated citation supporting a local fact is not a defect. Do not demand multiple citations per sentence. |
| Contribution and praise | “Novel”, “transformative”, “robust”, or similar language claims a benefit or scope that is not specified or supported in examined material. | Technical robustness and statistical significance can have exact meanings. Their presence alone is no finding. |
| Audience and abstraction | An abstract noun chain hides the operation or responsibility readers need, or unexplained terms obstruct the specified audience. | Formal register, specialist terms, passive procedures and concise non-native English may be appropriate. |
| Requested artifact fit | The artifact omits a supplied requirement or substitutes internal working-status boilerplate for the account readers were promised. | Do not delete genuine material uncertainty, AI-use disclosure or an unresolved compliance constraint to make a final artifact appear complete. |

## Acceptance and negative cases

These are invented review exercises, not research results. A maintainer can exercise them without manuscript edits; record actual outcomes separately rather than declaring these examples executed tests.

- **Clean technical passage:** “We use a robust estimator to reduce sensitivity to outliers. The difference was statistically significant at the prespecified threshold.” With appropriate method/result context, neither adjective is a defect. If support is absent from the reviewed scope, mark it unverified rather than ban the words.
- **Intentional punctuation:** “The control group—defined before recruitment—received the same instructions.” One purposeful dash pair needs no quota-based finding. Preserve a permitted consistent convention.
- **Meaningful repetition:** “Retention was measured after seven days. Retention was the primary outcome.” Reusing the construct name avoids a potentially ambiguous synonym. Do not force variation.
- **Procedure and uncertainty:** “Responses were coded independently by two raters. The association may reflect prior experience.” Passive voice and the hedge can be appropriate; do not replace them with an unsupported actor or causal conclusion.
- **Concise non-native English:** “We compare two methods on the same data.” This is clear, sufficient English; do not add flourish or infer origin.
- **Protected material:** An accurately attributed quotation containing “delve”, code named `robust_score`, and mathematical negation remain unchanged. Review surrounding explanation if necessary; do not alter evidence or executable meaning.
- **Demonstrated reasoning gap:** “Students using the tool had higher scores. Therefore, the tool caused learning.” Flag the unsupported causal transition unless the examined design supports it. Suggest checking the design and narrowing the inference, preserving the actual comparison and uncertainty; do not invent an experiment.
- **Missing evidence:** A supplied excerpt says “All participants improved by 40%”, but neither source nor data are supplied. Record the exact claim and the missing denominator/comparison/support as applicable. Do not declare the percentage false or verified.
- **Structured disclosure:** The author asks for Tool/principle, Workflow, Controls and Samples, but the appendix gives three broad assistance paragraphs plus “author/course confirmation pending”. Compare the actual appendix with the supplied format: identify missing stage-level AI and human actions, checks and representative samples. Suggest structuring supported records through [generative-ai-use](../generative-ai-use/SKILL.md); do not compose declarations during review. Internal task-status boilerplate can belong in the use record/handoff when the submission does not require it; material uncertainty or required disclosure stays explicit. Never fabricate author checks, policy permission or sample provenance.
- **Already adequate passage:** If context supplies the needed relationship and no meaningful reader problem remains, report no actionable finding. A long report is not an acceptance criterion.

## Research rationale, not universal thresholds

| Source and precise locator | What informs this review; limit |
| --- | --- |
| Shaib et al., [Measuring AI “Slop” in Text](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19163v3), version 3, revised 16 October 2026, Table 1, Sections 4 and 7 | The taxonomy distinguishes information utility, information quality and style. Substantial annotator disagreement and incomplete agreement of automated metrics/judges with humans motivate explaining observable effects. This preprint studies English news/QA with 19 expert definitions and three annotators; it supplies no universal academic cutoff or authorship test. |
| Reinhart et al., [Do LLMs write like humans? Variation in grammatical and rhetorical styles](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2422455122), 2025, Materials and Methods; Results, “Differences in Style and Vocabulary” and “Generalization Across Corpora” | Six GPT-4o/Llama 3 variants show model- and genre-dependent grammatical/rhetorical patterns. This motivates attention to audience and overloaded constructions, not a universal banned-word rule or diagnosis of an individual writer. |
| Oh et al., [Science or Slop?: Benchmarking and Mitigating Scientific Slop in AI-Generated Papers](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.00531v1), version 1, Sections 3, 4 and 6, Table 1 | This preprint examines scientific reasoning connections, redundancy, citation isolation, figure exposition and evidence gaps across 390 paired AI papers, mostly computer science. It motivates checking actual argumentative connections against experiment records; it does not require every paper to share a shape, prove all drafts poor, or justify inventing missing cross-references. |
| Liang et al., [GPT detectors are biased against non-native English writers](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10382961/), 2023, “GPT detectors exhibit bias against non-native English authors” and “Mitigating bias through linguistic diversity enhancement of non-native samples” | Seven older detectors were evaluated with 91 TOEFL essays and 88 US eighth-grade essays; the source groups differ. This supports caution about linguistic bias within that study, not claims about every current detector. The skill's prohibition on authorship verdicts and evasion is a review boundary. |

These sources do not authorize detectors or external manuscript uploads. They do not establish that plain, formal, conventional or non-native writing is low quality. Evaluate what the actual passage does for its reader.
