# Study-design workflow

Maintain original, reusable instructions for developing and checking research designs here. The skill produces methods records; it does not execute a study, choose consequential researcher decisions, or register a protocol externally.

Keep the entrypoint usable by explicit path in either assistant. Supporting templates must accommodate quantitative, qualitative and conceptual work without forcing irrelevant statistical requirements. Preserve proposed versus completed work, source locators, unresolved choices and dated amendments. Project-specific designs belong in `docs/methods/`, not this folder. Validate structure and representative behavior separately.
