# Research diagrams skill folder

`SKILL.md` is the portable entrypoint for creating evidence-aware Mermaid diagrams in canonical research Markdown. Keep this folder limited to the reusable diagram workflow and supporting resources that have a repeated need; research-specific figures and evidence belong in the appropriate `docs/` or `submissions/` subfolder.

Maintain English, provider-neutral instructions with relative links. Preserve a clear boundary between supported findings, interpretation, and proposed work. Validate the skill structure and any added resource links before handoff; check diagram rendering separately when a target renderer is available.
