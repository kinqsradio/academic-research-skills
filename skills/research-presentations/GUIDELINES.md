# Research presentation workflow

Maintain original, project-neutral instructions for academic slide creation, editing, and review. This folder holds procedures and reusable planning aids, not decks, research evidence, or review records.

- [SKILL.md](SKILL.md): entrypoint and delivery checks.
- [STORYBOARD_TEMPLATE.md](STORYBOARD_TEMPLATE.md): adaptable Markdown slide source and notes.
- [DESIGN_GUIDANCE.md](DESIGN_GUIDANCE.md): design decisions, supporting sources, and evidence limits.

Keep the skill usable by explicit path in either assistant. Use the current host's available presentation capabilities; do not require one editor, proprietary API, or provider. Keep the parent [skill index](../GUIDELINES.md) current. Validate structure separately from rendered output and research claims.
