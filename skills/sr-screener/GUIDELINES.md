# Study-screening skill guidelines

Maintain original English instructions for eligibility decisions and auditable selection reporting. `SKILL.md` defines the workflow; `decision-record.md` defines the minimum record. Keep human interpretation, missing information and runtime failures visible.

Store study files in `papers/`, screening records in a purpose-based `docs/literature/` subfolder and requested review artifacts in a purpose-based `submissions/reviews/` subfolder. Do not store research outputs inside the skill. Keep both Codex and Claude compatibility through capability descriptions. Add no runtime scripts or proprietary schema without a demonstrated need; every new subfolder needs `GUIDELINES.md`.

Use the canonical [paper guidance](../../papers/GUIDELINES.md), [download register](../../papers/DOWNLOADS.md), and [reference catalog](../../papers/REFERENCES.md) for source identity and provenance. Keep stable paper IDs separate from artifact-specific IEEE citation numbers. Update relevant supporting records when changing this workflow. The lead coordinates and checks scoped worker outputs; workers do not delegate further. Human-selected lead model and effort remain outside subagent routing limits.
